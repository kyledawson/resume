Increasingly focused on mathematical optimization for the energy sector --- directing a large-scale budget-constrained MILP for grid reliability and prototyping wholesale-market (ERCOT) optimization. Skilled at partnering with product, engineering, and commercial stakeholders to translate complex problems into rigorous models and shipped software, while setting technical direction and mentoring scientists and engineers.
